Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app:

  • Open the Journal tab and tap the person icon at the top.
  • Tap Account, then Delete Account, and confirm.

This permanently deletes your account and all of your recipes, folders, photos, and chats. It cannot be undone. You can also ask us to delete your account by emailing support@trychefy.com from the email address on your account. Our Privacy Policy explains what happens to your data after deletion.
